# Diogo Infante

Product Engineer working across engineering, design, and product. I build the part of the interface you feel but never see: the spring, the snap, the moment of feedback that decides whether software feels alive or dead.

For four years I owned the most interaction-heavy features at [Play](https://apps.apple.com/app/play-tool), a native iOS design tool, leading them from research and scoping through prototyping, product integration, release, and post-launch iteration. I partnered with the CPO and design team to build capabilities like audio, haptics, charts, IMU, maps, and Rive animations, holding to one rule: creative tools have to feel invisible enough to never break a designer's flow. That craft earned customers like Duolingo, Strava, and Tinder, and helped Play win a [2025 Apple Design Award](https://developer.apple.com/design/awards/2025/).

Mechatronics / Control & Automation / Robotics Engineering (PUC-Rio), which gives me strong mathematical footing and a habit of systems thinking. [Apple Developer Academy](https://developer.apple.com/academies/) alum. Based in Rio de Janeiro.

### What I'm working on

- Native iOS work in Swift: audio, motion, shaders, and haptics, with an emphasis on high-craft interaction.

- AI product engineering: RAG, agents, and evals, oriented toward shipping over research.

- Interfaces and tools that sit at the edge of hardware and software.
